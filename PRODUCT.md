# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People running their own Chimera server who need to get a Windows or Android
client talking to it without copying hex fields by hand—often from a chat
message on another device.

## Product Purpose

A self-hosted research VPN client. Success is: paste or tap one invite, hit
connect, see a live tunnel. Manual seed/PSK fields exist as a fallback, not
the default path.

## Brand Personality

Spare, precise, nocturnal. Operator tool, not a store-shelf VPN brand.

## Anti-references

GitHub Settings dark theme. Default Material form stacks. Consumer VPN neon
ads. SaaS metric-card dashboards. Gradient-text “security” landing pages.

## Design Principles

- The invite is the product; hex fields stay collapsed until asked for.
- One primary action: connect or disconnect.
- Never echo secrets into logs, toasts, or traffic labels.
- Familiar controls over invented chrome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA for text contrast. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables decorative
motion. Invite paste works with a hardware keyboard; tap targets ≥44px on
Android.
